## What was built

- **BE-19.1 Migration `0022_credit_domain`** (down_revision 0021, tag `0022_credit_domain`):
  - (a) Seeds `counterparty` v1 (4 fields) and `credit_exposure` v1 (16 fields) as ACTIVE for the default tenant, with the D-43 classifications and 3 tagged `schema_dependency` rows. The content hashes match `governance.contract_sha256`.
  - (b) The D-38 widening: the two `schema_code` CHECKs on `domain_projection` and `dataset_snapshot_member` are dropped and re-added under the same names, in one transaction. The migration declares `D01_EXCEPTION = "D-38"`.
  - (c) Creates `counterparty`, `counterparty_version`, `credit_exposure` and `credit_exposure_version`.
    - All four are append-only, TRUNCATE included.
    - They carry the 0017 insert checks: same tenant, same source, and each version repeats its canonical record's details.
    - Column types: NUMERIC amounts, DATE dates, INTEGER days past due (DPD), BOOLEAN default flag.
  - (d) Creates `credit_vocabulary`, append-only, with 19 tagged rows: G01 to G08 plus D, 4 segments, 3 facility types and 3 collateral types.
  - The downgrade refuses while any credit row, credit projection, snapshot member, import, source contract, mapping, proposal or later schema version uses the new codes or schemas. Otherwise it restores the narrow CHECKs and removes only the tagged rows.
- **BE-19.2 Synthetic samples.**
  - `samples/synthetic/counterparty.csv` has 8 rows across 4 segments.
  - `credit_exposure.csv` has 14 rows in EUR and USD.
  - Both are generated by `scripts/make_synthetic_samples.py`, and `SHA256SUMS` is updated.
  - The cases cover: a revolver with an undrawn amount, a guarantee, DPD 0/35/95, `default_flag`, CASH, REAL_ESTATE and unmapped collateral, an unknown origination grade, a passed maturity, and a counterparty key missing from the counterparty file.
  - The 3-notch case is EXP-0008 / CP-0008, originated at G02 (`credit_exposure.csv:10`). Its downgrade is completed by a G05 rating in Phase 20.
- **BE-19.3 Projector.**
  - The two credit specs sit under `projector_version` 1. `ColumnKind` gains INTEGER and BOOLEAN, and canonical integer and boolean text is parsed strictly.
  - A `country` that is not 2 capital letters is stored empty and counted as UNKNOWN_VALUE. Vocabulary fields are kept as given; values outside the vocabulary are counted as UNKNOWN_VALUE.
  - Setting `CREDIT_PROJECTION_ENABLED` to false gives 409 `projection_unsupported` for both codes, audited as DENIED.
  - The three Feature 03 specs are untouched.
- **BE-19.4 Snapshots.**
  - Snapshots accept the two codes, and `ambiguous_keys` now also covers counterparty and credit_exposure keys.
  - Feature 03 snapshot hashing is unchanged.
- **BE-19.5 Reads.** Five routes, all `domain:read`:
  - `GET /credit-vocabularies`
  - `GET /counterparties`: paginated, filtered by segment, country, source and import
  - `GET /counterparties/{id}`: the counterparty's versions, plus exposure versions matching its business key across the tenant
  - `GET /credit-exposures` and `GET /credit-exposures/{id}`
  - Masking follows the classification of the pinned field (D-43 §1). The payload hash is masked unless the caller is cleared for every field. Another tenant's id gets the same 404 as a random id.
- **BE-19.6 Contract.** `openapi.json` was exported first. Against HEAD it has 5 new paths and 11 new schemas, nothing removed, and `DomainSchemaCode` is the only changed schema. **The RETENTION.md rows and the ARCHITECTURE.md update are not done** (see Open items).
- **FE-19.1 Counterparties tab** under `/domain` (permission `domain:read`):
  - The catalogue page `/domain/counterparties` has the counterparty list with filters, plus a credit-exposure list whose filters carry the `exp_` prefix. It also shows the vocabularies with the "Provisional / illustrative" badge and a default-grade badge.
  - Counterparty detail shows every version with its lineage, plus the exposures that name the counterparty.
  - Exposure detail (`/domain/counterparties/exposures/{id}`) shows every field of every version, with lineage and a link to `/ingestion/imports/{id}` labelled "Import xxxx · row N".
  - Uses `MaskedValue` and generated types only.
  - `NEXT_PUBLIC_CREDIT_DOMAIN_UI=off` removes the tab, makes the routes 404 (CreditGate) and restores the Phase 18 `/domain` exactly.
  - The `scenarios` and `reports` placeholders now read "Scheduled for WS4 (planned)".
- **FE-19.2 "Project to domain".**
  - Offers `counterparty` and `credit_exposure`, taken from the generated `DomainSchemaCode` enum, and hides them when the credit UI flag is off.
  - The summary labels a credit projection as seeded, illustrative and provisional.
  - The browse link goes to `/domain/counterparties?import_id=…` for counterparty imports; credit_exposure imports get no link.
  - The server's 409 `projection_unsupported` is shown with the existing refusal title.

  2. `service.py:94` / `0022_credit_domain.py:455`: a negative `days_past_due` is accepted and stored. Either count it as UNKNOWN_VALUE and store NULL, or add `CHECK (days_past_due IS NULL OR days_past_due >= 0)`. The CHECK is still additive because the table is new.   5. `CounterpartyCatalogue.tsx:61,166,342`: both lists build their memo key from the whole query string, so changing a filter in one list refetches the other and sends it back to page 1. Build the key from each list's own prefixed parameters.
